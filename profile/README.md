## What we do

Yellowhead Digital is a forensic marketing-infrastructure practice. We work across the full arc, not just the audit:

- **Diagnose** — a forensic audit scores your infrastructure across ten pillars and compares what you *believe* about your setup against what independent analysis detects. The gap between the two is where the real findings live; the composite is the **MIDAS score**.
- **Architect** — from the findings we design the systems architecture: blueprints for tracking, consent, governance, and data flow — then build and configure them.
- **Operate** — ongoing accountability so the systems you own keep doing what they're meant to, and drift gets caught before it becomes a gap.

## How we build — Spec-Driven Development

We write the blueprint before touching the stack. Every system starts as a specification — intent, constraints, decisions, success criteria — kept as a living document, not an artifact that gathers dust. It's the discipline behind both our client builds and this platform.

It's also why our conceptual methodology is open while the algorithmic layer stays reserved: a system you can't understand is a system you can't trust, audit, or hand off.
